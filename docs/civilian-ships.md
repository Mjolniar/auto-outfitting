# Civilian ships

Freighters, tankers, liners, salvage rigs and survey ships never fight if you can help it, so guns, vents and capacitors on them are wasted OP. With **Civilian ships: fit for the campaign** on (the default), they get a campaign fit instead, in a single Optimal fit and in Optimize fleet.

## What they get

1. **Their own job first:**
   - freighters: Expanded Cargo Holds
   - tankers: Auxiliary Fuel Tanks
   - liners and transports: Additional Berthing
   - salvage and survey ships: Surveying Equipment
2. **Treasure Hunt Package**, if you have the Treasure Hunt mod. Getting nanoforges and other colony items early is worth a lot, and it adds up across the fleet.
3. Then, as the limit allows: burn speed (Augmented Drive Field, or a mod's version of it), Insulated Engine Assembly, High Resolution Sensors, Surveying Equipment, Efficiency Overhaul, Solar Shielding and Militarized Subsystems. Campaign hullmods from mods it doesn't know come after those.
4. **Whatever OP is left** goes to Reinforced Bulkheads (more hull, and a disabled ship is almost always recoverable), then Blast Doors (more hull, fewer crew lost).

No weapons, vents or capacitors. In Optimize fleet, their weapons and LPCs go back into the stock for your warships.

## The logistics limit

The game only lets a ship carry two logistics hullmods (some skills raise that). Built-in hullmods don't count against it, stock or s-mod, so an Atlas with cargo holds built in can still take two more.

Logistics hullmods can only be added at a spaceport. Away from one, the ones already on the ship stay where they are.

## Building in fleet-wide hullmods

Some hullmods help the whole fleet, not just the ship they're on. Civilian ships that stay in your fleet for good are the best place to build those in, since you'll have them all game.

With **Build in s-mods** ticked in the Autofit panel, and the **Civilian ships: build in fleet-wide hullmods** setting on (the default), these ships build them in:
- cruisers and capitals, like the Atlas, Prometheus and Colossus;
- salvage rigs, of any size.

In this order:

| Hullmod | Why |
|---|---|
| Treasure Hunt Package | 1, 2, 4 or 8 points by hull size, doubled as an s-mod, and it adds up across the fleet. |
| Surveying Equipment | 5, 10, 20 or 40 by hull size, doubled as an s-mod, also adds up across the fleet. |
| High Resolution Sensors | Adds to the fleet's sensor range, and built in it frees a logistics slot. |
| Burn speed | The fleet moves at its slowest ship, which is usually a freighter. |

If the ship already has one of these as a normal hullmod, it just gets built in. Each one costs a story point, only when you press Confirm (or Apply to all ships), and never below the story points you keep in reserve. See [S-mods and story points](s-mods.md).

Warships don't get Treasure Hunt Package, built in or not. It does nothing in a fight.

## Turning it off

- **Civilian ships: fit for the campaign** off: civilian ships get a normal combat fit like everything else.
- **Civilian ships: build in fleet-wide hullmods** off: they still get the campaign fit, just no s-mods.
